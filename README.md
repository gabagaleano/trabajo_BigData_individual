I'm Gabriela Galeano  (gabagaleano@gmail.com), and I'm the author of this web.

The web is titled DO PEOPLE EVEN RIOT? As an ironic and snarky remark on the fact that yes. People riot. And we do so despite, despite, despite. 

In my work I explore the different forms of police violence and brutality that protesters worldwide experience as well as the general trends in fatalities' numbers. I dive deeper into the cases of Venezuela (my hometown), and Hong Kong (not well-achieved as I couldn't find adequate data).
